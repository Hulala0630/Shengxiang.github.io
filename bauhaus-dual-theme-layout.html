Industrial Fault Simulation & Training

A repeatable training environment that turns production-line faults into safe, observable exercises across PLC, simulation and HMI layers.

  • Observe propagation across the chain
  • Reset and repeat without production risk
PLCOPC UAHMI
PROJECT CONTEXT

WHY IT MATTERS

Real production equipment is too costly and risky for repeated fault training. I helped structure abnormal states as reusable exercises so operators can understand symptoms, signal paths and recovery logic before touching the line.

  • Layered fault behavior across automation, simulation and interface states.
  • Designed for repeatable training, onboarding and engineering communication.
  • BMW-related figures and confidential implementation details remain deliberately generalized.

RESEARCH METHOD

FROM MOTION TO ACTIONABLE FEEDBACK

The evaluation pipeline extracts joint angles and temporal features from supplier-provided skeleton estimates, compares them with a reference motion and converts the difference into a multi-dimensional score.

  • Shoulder, elbow, wrist, waist, knee and ankle features.
  • Frame-level signals combined into interpretable movement dimensions.
  • Feedback is phrased as a concrete correction instead of a single opaque score.
